# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI


## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data and provide a comprehensive view of business performance through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

The dashboard helps users understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability and net profit
- Customer and order performance
- Product and category performance
- Sales by channel
- Payment method analysis
- Quantity sold
- Customer ratings
- Order status
- Sales representative performance
- Geographical sales performance
- Monthly and yearly trends

The project converts raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales and profitability.
2. Monitor important business KPIs.

4. Analyze sales performance across different channels.
5. Understand customer and order behavior.
6. Compare performance across cities and sales representatives.
7. Analyze payment methods and order status.
8. Track monthly and yearly business trends.
9. Provide interactive filtering for better exploration.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. 🏠 Home

The Home page acts as the navigation interface for the dashboard and provides access to the different analytical sections of the report.

### 2. 📈 Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level view of business performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Net Profit
- Delivered Orders
- Returned Orders

**Analysis includes:**
- Sales by Sales Channel
- Profit by Month
- Net Profit by Category
- Sales by Customer
- City/Sales Representative performance

**Filters:**
- City
- Segment
- Quarter
- Year

### 3. 🛍️ Category & Payment Method

This page focuses on product, category, profitability, quantity, and payment-method analysis.

**Analysis includes:**
- Product-level sales
- Product profitability
- Quantity by sub-category
- Sales by category
- Profit by category
- Net Profit Margin by category
- Payment Method analysis

**Filters:**
- Payment Method
- Segment
- Quarter
- Year

### 4. 🌎 City & Month

This page focuses on geographical, customer, order, and monthly analysis.

**Analysis includes:**
- Sales by customer segment
- Quantity by category
- Customer ratings by payment method

- Sales by sub-category
- City-level filtering

**Filters:**
- City
